## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-02 em dash in rendered UI text** — `apps/web/src/components/MagicTerminal.tsx:61`

   `'bash — 80x24'` (terminal chrome label). Same rule. Fix: `bash · 80x24` or `bash (80x24)`.

   real status source. An empty claim is better than an invented one.

   security work's Phase 6c. Listed here for completeness; no further action.

5. **R-37 no design direction** — repo has no `DESIGN.md` or brand guide. Every UI change
   is a draft without direction and trends toward generic. Fix: author a `DESIGN.md`
